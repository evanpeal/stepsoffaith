Steps to Faith — a guided walk through the Bible

Steps to Faith is a free Bible app that walks you through Scripture one short lesson at a time, starting in Genesis and running all the way to Revelation. Every lesson gives you a passage to read, a few questions to check what you understood, and a deeper look at what it means.

What you can do

  • Follow the path — short lessons through every book of the Bible, in order or jump to any book you want.
  • Read daily — a verse and devotional every morning, with a streak to keep you coming back.
  • Test what you know — Bible quizzes, a daily word game, gems, pearls and badges to earn.
  • Search by how you feel — verses for anxiety, grief, doubt, guilt, loneliness or thankfulness.
  • Go deeper — deep studies on each book, character studies, a full Bible timeline and reading plans.
  • Walk with others — join the Upper Room with friends or a study group and share prayer requests.

Free to use, works on your phone or computer, and your progress saves across every device once you make an account.
